I 🩵

You loved someone who loved the version of you
that bent, not the one who stood tall.

And when they left, you thought the fault was yours —
that you weren't enough, that you asked for too much.
But the truth is quieter:
they didn't know how to stay.

Healing isn't forgetting.
It's learning to hold your own hand
on the nights no one else does.
It's realizing you were never the problem —
you were just loved by someone
who didn't know how to keep you.

You are whole now.
Not because someone completed you,
but because you stopped waiting for them to.

— carlitos

LOVE 🌧️

There is a particular silence
that follows a love that broke you —
not the loud kind,
but the kind that settles in your ribs
when you stop explaining yourself to ghosts.

You don't need closure from them.
Closure is the morning you wake up
and their name doesn't taste like salt anymore.
It's the day you stop rehearsing conversations
you'll never have.

You are allowed to outgrow the pain.
You are allowed to be soft again.
You are allowed to love someone
who stays the first time.

— carlitos

YOU ✨

One day, someone will love you
without making you beg for the basics.
They will not call you "too much."
They will call you "mine."

And you'll look back at this version of you —
the one who cried on bathroom floors,
who rewrote texts ten times before sending,
who loved people who couldn't stay —
and you'll thank her.

Because she survived the love that wasn't enough
so you could recognize the one that is.

You're not behind.
You're not broken.
You're becoming.

— carlitos
